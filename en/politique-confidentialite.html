1. Preamble

PARTNERS attaches great importance to the protection of your personal data. This privacy policy informs you of how we collect, use and protect your data when you browse the www.partners-supply.com website.

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR — EU 2016/679) and the French Data Protection Act (Law No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978, as amended), we are committed to ensuring secure and transparent processing of your data.

3. Data Collected

We may collect the following data when you use our website or contact us :

  • Identification data : last name, first name, company, job title
  • Contact details : email address, telephone number, postal address
  • Browsing data : pages visited, visit duration, browser used (via analytical cookies)
  • Correspondence data : messages exchanged via the contact form or email

4. Purposes of Processing

Your data is collected for the following purposes :

  • Responding to your requests for quotes and information
  • Managing the commercial relationship (orders, invoices, deliveries)
  • Improving our website and user experience
  • Complying with our legal and regulatory obligations
  • Sending you commercial information with your consent

5. Legal Basis for Processing

The processing of your data is based on :

  • Performance of a contract (quotes, orders, services)
  • Your consent (cookies, newsletters)
  • Our legitimate interest (website improvement, customer relationship management)
  • A legal obligation (invoicing, accounting)

6. Retention Period

Your data is retained for the period necessary for the purposes for which it was collected :

  • Data relating to the commercial relationship : 5 years after the end of the relationship
  • Browsing data (cookies) : 13 months maximum
  • Accounting data : 10 years (legal obligation)

7. Data Recipients

Your data is intended for PARTNERS' internal departments. It is not transmitted to third parties without your consent, except :

  • Technical service providers (hosting, transport, payment) within the strict scope of their assignment
  • Legal authorities in the event of a legal obligation

8. Data Security

We implement all appropriate technical and organizational measures to guarantee the security and confidentiality of your personal data against any unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure.

9. Your Rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the following rights :

  • Right of access — obtain a copy of your data
  • Right of rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request the deletion of your data
  • Right to restriction — limit the processing of your data
  • Right to data portability — retrieve your data in a structured format
  • Right to object — object to the processing of your data

To exercise your rights, contact us at : infos@partners-supply.com. We undertake to respond within one month. In the event of a dispute, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

10. Cookies

Our website uses technical cookies necessary for its operation. Analytical cookies (Google Analytics) may be used to measure audience, with your prior consent. You can configure your preferences at any time via our cookie banner or your browser settings.

11. Policy Amendments

This privacy policy may be amended at any time. The updated version is published on this page with the date of amendment. We invite you to consult it regularly.
