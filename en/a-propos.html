Our History

2006

Creation of Partners

Founded within the international transport group TRANSPROJETS, PARTNERS is a procurement firm specializing in the supply of genuine automotive and heavy-duty spare parts for French and foreign vehicles.

2013

Mining Oil & Gas Division

Our MINING OIL & GAS division specifically addresses the needs of mining and oil companies in Africa and the Middle East, strengthening our presence in international markets.

2026

International Presence

Based south of Paris, the company has offices and warehouses enabling it to group and consolidate spare parts, equipment and materials for its clients across Africa, Europe and the Middle East.

Our areas of activity include the automotive, heavy-duty and public works industries. Our European and international clientele has allowed us to forge very close ties with numerous manufacturers and distribution networks.

  • Distribution of new and used vehicles and machinery
  • Mining exploration and exploitation equipment
  • Specific equipment for Oil & Gas projects
  • Spare parts and tooling for this equipment
